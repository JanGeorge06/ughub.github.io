Child Safety and CSAE Policy

UGC Hub is committed to preventing the creation, sharing, or distribution of any content related to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Zero Tolerance Policy

We strictly prohibit any content or behavior that involves child sexual abuse material, exploitation, grooming, or endangerment of minors.

Detection and Enforcement

We use a combination of automated systems and user reporting mechanisms to detect and remove violating content.

Reporting

Users can report suspicious or abusive content directly within the app. Reports are reviewed promptly and action is taken immediately when violations are found.

Enforcement Actions

  • Immediate removal of violating content
  • Account suspension or permanent ban
  • Escalation to relevant authorities when required

Contact

For safety concerns, contact: support@yourdomain.com
