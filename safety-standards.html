Child Safety & CSAE Standards

Last updated: September 17, 2026

These Child Safety and CSAE Standards govern safety requirements on UGC Hub and explain our rules concerning minors, prohibited content, and reporting.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish sexual contact with a minor.
  • Requests, offers, distribution, or facilitation of CSAE material.
  • Content or behavior that encourages or facilitates the abuse or exploitation of minors.

Moderation and Administrative Enforcement

UGC Hub maintains a restricted administrative moderation portal used by authorized administrators and moderators to review and manage user accounts and platform content. Depending on the circumstances, authorized moderators may block or unblock accounts, remove or restore campaigns or application content, remove profile photographs, and record moderation actions in an audit log. Access to the portal is restricted to authorized personnel and is used to enforce these standards, protect users, and maintain platform safety.

\n

Enforcement

UGC Hub may remove violating material, restrict access, suspend accounts, or permanently terminate accounts. We may preserve information and cooperate with law enforcement or other competent authorities where required by law or appropriate to address suspected CSAE.

Reporting Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected CSAE, child endangerment, or other serious safety concerns by contacting info@ugchub.app. Include the relevant account or content information when it is safe and lawful to do so. Do not send illegal child sexual abuse material as an attachment.

Handling Reports

Safety reports are reviewed and handled according to their nature and urgency. Appropriate action may include restricting content or accounts, preserving relevant information, and escalating matters to competent authorities where required or appropriate.

Prevention

UGC Hub requires users to be adults and prohibits CSAE through its Terms of Service. Users are required to agree to the Terms before creating an account.
