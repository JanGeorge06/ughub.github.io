UGC Hub connects content creators and brands for marketing campaigns. This Privacy Policy explains the personal information processed by the UGC Hub mobile application and related services.

1. Information You Provide

  • Account information: full name, username, email address, password credentials handled by our authentication provider, phone number, country, and account role (creator or brand).
  • Profile information: biography, profile photograph, selected creator categories, and social-media links such as Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook links.
  • Campaign information: campaign titles, descriptions, budgets, dates, requirements, campaign photographs, brand association, and campaign status.
  • Application information: campaign applications, application messages, application status, dates, and related creator/brand identifiers.
  • Support communications: information you choose to provide when contacting UGC Hub Support.

2. Photos and Files

The current application allows users to select and upload profile photographs. Uploaded profile photographs are stored in UGC Hub's Supabase Storage infrastructure. The application requests camera and storage/media permissions where required by the device and operating system to support media selection. Do not upload material that you do not have permission to use.

3. Information Collected Automatically

The Android application includes Firebase Performance Monitoring and may process technical and performance information needed to diagnose and improve app performance. The application also necessarily processes basic technical information associated with network requests and authentication sessions. We do not state that the app collects a specific device identifier, precise location, contacts, SMS, or advertising ID unless such collection is actually enabled.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create and authenticate accounts.
  • Maintain user profiles.
  • Connect creators and brands.
  • Publish and manage campaigns.
  • Process campaign applications.
  • Display appropriate public profile information to other users.
  • Store and display profile photographs and campaign media.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain service security and reliability.
  • Diagnose crashes and performance problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

5. Information Visible to Other Users

The application exposes selected profile information through public profile views. Depending on the account, this can include name, username, role, biography, profile photograph, country, selected social-media links, and account creation date. Email address and phone number are kept on the user's private profile and are not included in the public profile view used for other users.

6. Campaign and Application Visibility

Campaign information is available to authenticated users as part of the campaign marketplace. Application information is accessible to the participating creator and brand according to the application's access controls.

7. Service Providers

UGC Hub uses third-party infrastructure and services, including Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage, and Firebase Performance Monitoring for app performance monitoring. These providers process information according to their applicable service terms and privacy practices.

8. Data Storage and Security

UGC Hub uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. Supabase stores the application's database and uploaded profile media. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate UGC Hub, maintain account and campaign records, provide support, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized where technically and legally appropriate.

10. Account Deletion

Users can delete their account from inside the application. The deletion operation removes the user's authentication account and associated UGC Hub database records, including their profile, categories, campaigns they own, and associated campaign applications. See the Account Deletion page for instructions and retention exceptions.

Uploaded profile photographs are stored separately in object storage. Account deletion removes the associated database records, but media files should be considered subject to storage cleanup and may require verification if an upload cannot be mapped to an account record. Contact info@ugchub.app if you need confirmation concerning a specific uploaded file.

11. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise request information concerning your personal data. Contact info@ugchub.app to make a request.

12. Children's Privacy

UGC Hub is intended for adults aged 18 and older. The service is not intended for children. We do not knowingly permit children to create accounts. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

13. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries where UGC Hub or its service providers operate. Applicable safeguards depend on the relevant service provider and applicable law.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.
