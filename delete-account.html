Delete Your UGC Hub Account

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

UGC Hub respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to delete their account and associated personal data. You can do this yourself from inside the app, or by email if you prefer.

Delete Your Account in the App

This is the fastest method and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open UGC Hub and sign in.
  2. Go to My profile, from the side menu or the profile shortcut on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type your username to confirm, then tap Delete.

This cannot be undone. Your account is deleted straight away, and neither you nor we can restore it afterwards.

In-app deletion is available in UGC Hub version 1.0.3 and later. If you do not see the option, update the app from Google Play.

Delete Your Account by Email

If you cannot access the app, or you would rather we handled it, send an email to:

Email: info@ugchub.app
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please send it from, or include, the email address associated with your UGC Hub account so we can verify ownership. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days of receiving them.

What Is Deleted

Whichever method you use, deleting your account removes:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your profile information, including your name, username, bio, country, contact details and social links.
  • Your selected categories.
  • Campaigns you created, if you use UGC Hub as a brand, together with every application submitted to them.
  • Applications you submitted, if you use UGC Hub as a creator.
  • Your access to the UGC Hub platform.

Uploaded media, such as your profile photo, is removed as part of the same request. If you delete your account in the app and would like to confirm your uploaded files have been removed, email info@ugchub.app.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for:

  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes.
  • Resolution of disputes.
  • Enforcement of our Terms and Conditions.

Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for these purposes.
