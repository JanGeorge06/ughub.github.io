Delete Your UGHub Account

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

UGHub respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your UGHub account, please send an email to:

Email: info@ugchub.app
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your UGHub account so we can verify ownership and process your request.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

Upon successful verification of your request, we will:

  • Delete your UGHub account.
  • Delete your profile information.
  • Delete portfolio content and uploaded media associated with your account where applicable.
  • Remove your access to the UGHub platform.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for:

  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes.
  • Resolution of disputes.
  • Enforcement of our Terms and Conditions.

Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for these purposes.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days of receiving the request.

Future Self-Service Deletion Feature

UGHub is currently developing an in-app account deletion feature that will allow users to delete their accounts directly from within the application.

Until this feature becomes available, account deletion requests can be submitted by email at info@ugchub.app.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding account deletion, please contact:

Email: info@ugchub.app
