Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 17, 2026

UGC Hub provides an in-app account deletion option and an external deletion request method.

Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Sign in to UGC Hub.
  2. Open My Profile.
  3. Scroll to the Delete Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Type your username to confirm and complete the deletion.

The current application version includes this functionality. Deletion is intended to be permanent.

Delete Your Account by Email

Email: info@ugchub.app
Subject: Account Deletion Request

If you cannot access the application, contact us from the email associated with your account, or provide sufficient information for us to verify account ownership. We will process verified requests as soon as reasonably practicable.

What the In-App Deletion Operation Removes

  • Your Supabase authentication account.
  • Your UGC Hub profile.
  • Your selected creator categories.
  • Campaigns owned by your brand account.
  • Campaign applications associated with your account as creator or brand.
  • Your access to the platform.

Uploaded Media

Profile photographs are stored in Supabase Storage. The account deletion database operation removes the database reference to the profile photograph. Storage objects are maintained separately, so if you need confirmation that a particular uploaded file has been removed, contact info@ugchub.app with the relevant account information.

Data That May Be Retained

Information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal obligations, security and fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our Terms. Such information will only be retained for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.
